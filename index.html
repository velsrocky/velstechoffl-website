About VelsTech

I'm a developer and technology enthusiast who spends a questionable amount of time running Linux, breaking things, testing hardware, and experimenting with local AI.

VelsTech is where I document what actually works — including the things that didn't. Expect practical notes, benchmarks, and troubleshooting guides rather than theory dumps. The kind of stuff that helps the next time you're setting something up.

🧪 VelsTech Tested Every benchmark and hardware review gets a VelsTech Tested badge — with hardware, software versions, configuration, test methodology, results, problems encountered, and a final verdict. Failures are valuable content too.
