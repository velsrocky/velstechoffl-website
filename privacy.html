Privacy Policy

Last updated: Aug 24, 2026

This policy explains what information this Site collects and how it's used. The short version: we collect almost nothing, and we never sell data.

1. Information you provide

If you email us at hello@velstech.net, we receive whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to you and don't share it.

2. Information stored on your device

The Site uses your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference (light/dark) and accent color. This data stays on your device — it is not sent to us and is not accessible to the Site's operators.

3. No tracking

This Site does not use tracking cookies, analytics, fingerprinting, or advertising scripts. We don't build profiles and we don't follow you across the web.

4. Hosting and infrastructure

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. These providers may process standard technical data (such as IP addresses) as part of delivering and securing the Site. We don't control their logging practices; you can review their policies at GitHub and Cloudflare.

5. Third-party links

Articles may link to external sites. Once you leave this Site, this policy no longer applies — please check the privacy policy of any site you visit.

6. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Site evolves. Changes take effect when posted.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at hello@velstech.net.
